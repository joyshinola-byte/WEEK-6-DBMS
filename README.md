**WEEK 6 – PRODUCT REVIEW AND RATING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM**

**REVIEW REPORTS**


**Introduction**

Review reports are used to analyze customer feedback and product ratings. These reports help identify popular products,
highly rated products, and products that need improvement.

**1. Product Review Report**

This report displays the product name, total number of reviews, and average rating. It helps understand customer 
opinions about each product.

**2. Most Reviewed Products**
This report identifies products that have received the highest number of customer reviews. It helps find popular products.

**3. Highly Rated Products**

This report displays products with an average rating of 4 or above. It helps identify products that satisfy customers.

**4. Products Needing Improvement**

This report identifies products with an average rating below 3. It helps businesses understand which products
require improvement.

**5. Rating Distribution Report**

This report displays the number of 1-star, 2-star, 3-star, 4-star, and 5-star reviews. It helps analyze overall
customer satisfaction.

### Conclusion
The Review Reports help analyze customer feedback, measure product performance, and identify customer satisfaction levels. These reports support better product quality and business decisions.
